NEW IN V0.7
- Live transcript replaces the short rolling caption.
- Every thought grows word by word while it is spoken.
- A completed thought receives punctuation and starts a new line.
- The last 10 completed thoughts remain visible.
- Older thoughts gradually dim while the newest stays bright.
- Thought 11 removes the oldest line and reuses the space.
- CC: ON/OFF hides or reveals the transcript without stopping the voice.
- Turning CC back on resumes the visible transcript; hidden thoughts are not
  dumped onto the screen afterwards.
- False starts, “eh”, “no”, and corrected thoughts appear exactly when spoken.
- Breaths, coughs, sighs and silence remain invisible.
- Sentence endings vary between:
    .
    ...
    ?
    —
- Continuous speech, suffix stitching, room presence and V0.6 timing remain.

This makes screenshots practical when the machine accidentally says
something wise, obscene, or both.
