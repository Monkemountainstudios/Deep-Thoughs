DEEP THOUGHS — PHASE 2

This version discovers the vocabulary and speaks one generated thought.

COPY THESE FILES TO THE REPOSITORY ROOT
  index.html
  style.css
  program.js

They should sit beside:
  audio/

HOW TO TEST
1. Push the three files.
2. Wait for GitHub Pages to update.
3. Open:
   https://monkemountainstudios.github.io/Deep-Thoughs/
4. Press THINK A THOUGHT.
5. The first press loads and decodes the audio library.
6. After that, THINK AGAIN generates another thought immediately.

WHAT IT CURRENTLY DOES
- Discovers all .ogg files automatically.
- Loads every recorded word.
- Uses several simple sentence frames.
- Allows stacked adjectives and adverbs.
- Occasionally creates compound noun phrases with “and”.
- Includes one deliberately damaged/non sequitur structure.
- Spaces the words with small irregular gaps.
- Sometimes ends with a breath.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET
- Infinite playback.
- The five-word moving subtitle buffer.
- Themes or semantic memory.
- Proper handling of every English inflection.
- Full poem/paragraph structures.

Those come after we hear whether the first voice has anything worth saying.
