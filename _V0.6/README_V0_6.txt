NEW IN V0.6
- START THINKING begins an endless stream of generated thoughts.
- STOP THINKING allows the current thought to finish, then stops.
- Thoughts are separated by variable silences:
    usually 1.7–3.8 seconds
    sometimes 3.5–6.5 seconds
    rarely 7–14 seconds
- Live rolling captions show each word when its audio begins.
- The caption remembers:
    10 words on wide screens
     8 words on medium screens
     6 words on narrow screens
- False starts, “eh”, “no”, and corrections now appear live too.
- Breaths, coughs and silence remain invisible in the caption.
- Ordinary programmed word gaps are now approximately 8–45 ms.
  The imperfect trimming of the recordings supplies extra natural variation.
- Larger pauses now occur mainly at phrase cues, connectors, stressed words,
  final words and deliberate dramatic moments.
- Plural suffix stitching from V0.5 remains intact.

The machine should now speak in phrases instead of considering every word
as a separate philosophical emergency.
