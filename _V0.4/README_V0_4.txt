NEW IN V0.4
- Phrase-level pitch arcs.
- One to three stressed words per thought.
- Stressed words receive subtle pitch, volume and timing emphasis.
- Pauses have grammatical reasons but remain randomized.
- Extra pauses around connectors, modals, verbs, interjections and final words.
- Occasional Shatner-grade dramatic pauses.
- Several delivery modes: reflective, oracle, solemn, certain, measured,
  story, dreamlike, odd and self-correcting.
- Small dark-room reverb shared by speech and breaths.
- Roughly 7% of thoughts begin, lose confidence, say something like
  “eh... no”, and start again.
- Natural breaths remain occasional rather than compulsory.

The visible sentence shows the final thought only.
The abandoned false start remains an audio surprise.
